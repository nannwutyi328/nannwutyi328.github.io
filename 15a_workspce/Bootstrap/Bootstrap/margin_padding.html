Bootstrap Margin

Margin adds space outside an element or outside of border.

Margin Classes
1. m-0: margin 0
2. m-1: small margin
3. m-2: medium margin
4. m-3: normal margin
5. m-4: large margin
6. m-5 extra large margin
Margin Directions
1. mt: margin top
2. mt: margin bottom
3. mt: margin left(start)
4. mt: margin right(end)
5. mt: margin left & right
6. mt: margin top & bottom

Bootstrap Padding

Padding addds space inside an element or space between content and border.

padding Classes
1. p-0: padding 0
2. p-1: small padding
3. p-2: medium padding
4. p-3: normal padding
5. p-4: large padding
6. p-5 extra large padding
padding Directions
1. pt: padding top
2. pt: padding bottom
3. pt: padding left(start)
4. pt: padding right(end)
5. pt: padding left & right
6. pt: padding top & bottom
